Purpose of Collection

We collect your information for specific, legitimate purposes — all directly related to delivering the services you request. Here is what we use your data for:

  • Service Delivery: To process business registrations, visa applications, and financial service requests.
  • Identity Verification: To confirm your identity as required by regulatory bodies.
  • Regulatory Compliance: To meet legal and regulatory obligations for document processing.
  • Communication: To provide updates on your application status and respond to inquiries.
  • Service Improvement: To evaluate and enhance our service delivery.
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent activities.
  • Record Keeping: To maintain accurate records for compliance and reference.

We never collect information without a clear purpose, and we never use your data for purposes unrelated to your service.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Complete Your Service: Your data remains accessible until your application is fully processed.

When your information is no longer required, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

As a user of our services, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.

  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing where applicable.
