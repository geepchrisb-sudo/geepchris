What is a Trademark?

A Trademark is a legally protected symbol, word, or design that distinguishes your business from competitors. It gives you exclusive rights to use your brand identity and prevents others from copying or imitating your logo, name, or slogan.

Registering a trademark turns your brand into an valuable business asset.

If you don't register your trademark, someone else can. Competitors can legally register your business name or logo and take ownership of your brand identity. This can lead to loss of customers, revenue, and lawsuits—all preventable with proper trademark registration.
