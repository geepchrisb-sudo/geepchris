7 Smart Ways to Find Cheaper Local & International Flights

GeepChris Bridge | 6 min read | Updated 2026
Back to Blog

The cheapest flight isn't always the one with the lowest advertised price. Knowing when to book, which dates to choose, and how flexible travel works can save you thousands.

Here are seven practical strategies travelers use to reduce flight costs without sacrificing convenience.

1. Book Earlier, Not Last Minute

Flight prices generally increase as departure dates approach. Booking 2-3 months in advance for international travel and 3-4 weeks ahead for domestic routes usually yields the best rates.

The sweet spot for international flights is typically 60-90 days before departure. Last-minute bookings often come with a premium.

2. Be Flexible With Dates

Mid-week departures (Tuesday and Wednesday) are often cheaper than weekend flights. If your schedule allows, shifting your travel by a day or two can make a significant difference.

3. Compare Nearby Airports

Flying into a secondary airport can sometimes save you money. For example, flying into Gatwick instead of Heathrow, or smaller regional airports, often comes with lower fares.

4. Travel During Off-Peak Seasons

Avoiding peak travel periods—Christmas, New Year, Easter, and school holidays—can dramatically reduce your fare. Off-peak travel also means fewer crowds and better availability.

5. Choose the Right Cabin Class

Premium economy can offer better value than business class for long-haul flights. Consider what you actually need—extra legroom, meals, or just the seat itself.

6. Group & Family Booking Tips

Some airlines offer discounts for group bookings or family packages. Always inquire about group rates when traveling with more than 5 people.

7. When to Use a Travel Agent

For complex itineraries, multi-city trips, or routes with multiple airlines, a travel agent can often find combinations that online booking tools miss.
